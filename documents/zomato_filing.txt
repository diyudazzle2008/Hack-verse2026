Zomato Limited – Q2 FY26 Earnings Summary

Zomato reported consolidated revenue of ₹4,850 crore for Q2 FY26, up 
14.7% quarter-on-quarter, driven primarily by growth in its quick 
commerce segment, Blinkit. Adjusted EBITDA margin narrowed to 1.2%, 
down from 2.8% in the prior quarter, due to aggressive investment in 
dark store expansion.

The company added 120 new Blinkit dark stores during the quarter, 
while food delivery gross order value grew 9.3% year-on-year. Customer 
acquisition costs rose amid intensifying competition in the quick 
commerce space.

Management indicated that profitability in the food delivery segment 
remains stable, while Blinkit is expected to reach breakeven within 
the next two to three quarters, contingent on continued scale-driven 
efficiency gains.
