Reliance Industries Limited – Q2 FY26 Earnings Summary

Reliance Industries reported consolidated revenue of ₹2,45,000 crore for 
Q2 FY26, up 8.2% year-on-year, driven primarily by growth in the retail 
and digital services segments. EBITDA margin improved to 17.3%, compared 
to 16.1% in the same quarter last year.

The company's debt-to-equity ratio stood at 0.35, down from 0.41, 
reflecting continued deleveraging efforts. Capital expenditure for the 
quarter was ₹18,200 crore, largely directed toward renewable energy 
infrastructure and 5G network expansion.

Management highlighted risks including volatility in crude oil prices 
and regulatory uncertainty in the telecom tariff structure, but 
expressed confidence in double-digit revenue growth for the full 
fiscal year.
