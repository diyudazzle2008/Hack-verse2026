HDFC Bank Limited – Q2 FY26 Earnings Summary

HDFC Bank reported net interest income of ₹30,200 crore for Q2 FY26, 
up 9.4% year-on-year, driven by steady growth in retail and SME loan 
disbursements. Gross non-performing assets (NPA) ratio stood at 1.3%, 
broadly stable compared to the previous quarter.

The bank's capital adequacy ratio improved to 18.6%, well above 
regulatory requirements, following continued balance sheet 
consolidation post-merger. Deposit growth was recorded at 11.2% 
year-on-year, with a healthy mix of low-cost current and savings 
account (CASA) deposits.

Management flagged margin pressure from rising deposit costs but 
expressed confidence in sustained loan book growth, particularly in 
the unsecured retail and MSME lending segments.
